Accessibility

Designing for every player

Accessibility runs through my work: readable text at every size and language, colorblind modes, and combat feedback that never relies on sound alone. On Hyper Scape, that work was reviewed by a Deaf player for Game Accessibility Nexus.

My contribution on Hyper Scape

  • Directional damage indicators and combat markers
  • Health bars and combat feedback (“combat comfort”)
  • In-game text rendering and font management
  • Motion design for the HUD
